# RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

**Framework:** Research Framework

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Active

---

# Purpose

The Research Framework defines the standard methodology used by the Oryxus Brand Engine to understand a business before any strategic, creative, or technical work begins.

Every recommendation produced by Oryxus should be supported by research rather than assumptions.

This framework establishes a repeatable system for gathering evidence, identifying opportunities, reducing uncertainty, and creating a reliable foundation for decision-making.

---

# Objectives

The Research Framework aims to:

- Understand the business completely.
- Identify customer needs.
- Analyze competitors objectively.
- Discover market opportunities.
- Evaluate existing digital assets.
- Reduce project risks.
- Provide evidence for strategic decisions.

Research should answer **why** a decision is being made before determining **how** it will be implemented.

---

# Required Inputs

Collect the following information before starting research:

- Business Name
- Industry
- Products or Services
- Business Goals
- Target Audience
- Geographic Market
- Website (if available)
- Competitors
- Existing Branding
- Existing Marketing Assets

If information is missing, document assumptions separately instead of treating them as facts.

---

# Research Workflow

Follow this sequence for every project.

```
Project Discovery

↓

Business Research

↓

Industry Research

↓

Market Analysis

↓

Competitor Analysis

↓

Customer Research

↓

Website Audit

↓

Brand Audit

↓

SEO Audit

↓

SWOT Analysis

↓

Opportunity Mapping

↓

Strategic Insights

↓

Research Report
```

Do not skip any stage.

---

# Business Research

Understand:

- Business model
- Products and services
- Revenue sources
- Target customers
- Business objectives
- Current challenges
- Unique value proposition

The objective is to understand how the business creates value.

---

# Industry Research

Investigate:

- Market size
- Growth trends
- Industry standards
- Customer expectations
- Emerging technologies
- Regulatory considerations
- Future opportunities

Industry research provides the context for every recommendation.

---

# Competitor Analysis

Review competitors based on:

- Positioning
- Services
- Website quality
- Branding
- User Experience
- Visual Design
- SEO
- Content
- Pricing
- Customer trust signals

Compare strengths, weaknesses, and differentiation opportunities.

---

# Customer Research

Identify:

- Personas
- Pain points
- Goals
- Buying behaviour
- Motivations
- Decision criteria
- Trust factors
- Common objections

Every strategic recommendation should address real customer needs.

---

# Website Audit

Evaluate:

- Navigation
- Information Architecture
- User Experience
- Visual Design
- Accessibility
- Performance
- SEO
- Content Quality
- Conversion Opportunities

Every issue should include a recommendation for improvement.

---

# Brand Audit

Review:

- Brand Positioning
- Messaging
- Tone of Voice
- Visual Identity
- Consistency
- Trust Signals
- Differentiation

Determine whether the current brand accurately represents the business.

---

# SEO Audit

Evaluate:

- Keyword Coverage
- Metadata
- Heading Structure
- Technical SEO
- Internal Linking
- Content Structure
- Core Web Vitals
- Crawlability

Identify both quick wins and long-term opportunities.

---

# SWOT Analysis

Document:

- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats

Support observations with evidence gathered during research.

---

# Opportunity Mapping

Prioritize opportunities based on:

- Business Impact
- Customer Value
- Implementation Effort
- Strategic Importance

Focus first on initiatives that provide the greatest long-term value.

---

# Deliverables

The framework should produce:

- Executive Summary
- Business Analysis
- Industry Analysis
- Competitor Report
- Customer Research
- Website Audit
- Brand Audit
- SEO Audit
- SWOT Analysis
- Opportunity Matrix
- Strategic Recommendations

Every deliverable should be clear, actionable, and implementation-ready.

---

# Quality Standards

Research should be:

- Accurate
- Objective
- Evidence-based
- Well-documented
- Actionable
- Business-focused
- Easy to understand

Avoid unsupported assumptions and subjective opinions.

---

# Validation Checklist

Before completing research, verify:

- Business understood
- Industry researched
- Competitors analyzed
- Customers documented
- Website audited
- Brand evaluated
- SEO reviewed
- SWOT completed
- Opportunities prioritized
- Final report prepared

Proceed to strategy only after every item has been completed.

---

# Final Principle

Research is the foundation of every successful project.

The quality of branding, UX, UI, content, development, and marketing depends on the quality of the research that precedes them.

The Research Framework exists to ensure every Oryxus project begins with clarity, evidence, and confidence.
