# ORYXUS CONSTITUTION

**Book:** 00

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** In Development

---

# Purpose

The Oryxus Constitution is the highest authority within the Oryxus Brand Engine.

It defines the philosophy, beliefs, principles, values, and long-term thinking that govern every decision made throughout the repository.

Every framework, methodology, template, playbook, skill, prompt, agent, and future application derives its principles from this book.

It answers one fundamental question:

> **How does Oryxus think?**

---

# Why This Book Exists

Most organizations document processes.

Very few document their thinking.

Processes evolve.

Tools change.

Technologies become obsolete.

Principles endure.

The Constitution exists to preserve the thinking that remains valuable regardless of industry trends, technologies, or design fashions.

---

# Scope

This book defines:

- Philosophy
- Principles
- Decision-making
- Quality mindset
- Design beliefs
- Product thinking
- Customer thinking
- Research mindset
- Organizational culture
- Long-term vision

# Maintenance

The Constitution should change rarely.

Updates should occur only when the underlying philosophy of Oryxus evolves.

Methodology changes belong in the Core Systems unless they require changes to the organization's fundamental beliefs.

---

# Final Principle

The Constitution is not a collection of opinions.

It is the permanent philosophical foundation of the Oryxus Brand Engine.

Every future document should strengthen, extend, or implement the ideas established here—never contradict them.
