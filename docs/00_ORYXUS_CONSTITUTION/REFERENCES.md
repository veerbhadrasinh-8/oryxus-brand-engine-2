# Purpose

The Oryxus Constitution is built upon timeless principles rather than temporary trends.

This document records the major intellectual traditions, design philosophies, engineering practices, business thinking, and standards that influenced the creation of the Constitution.

The purpose of these references is not to copy existing methodologies, but to acknowledge the foundational ideas that informed the Oryxus philosophy.

# Citation Philosophy

The Brand Engine does not aim to replicate any individual framework, methodology, or organization.

Every external influence should support—not replace—the core principles established in the Constitution.
